OSiRIS storage, and the HORUS computing service built on it, continue on a **best-effort basis through February 2027**. News and user information for both services are now posted at [horus-ci.org](https://horus-ci.org/).

<!--excerpt-->

OSiRIS has run in best-effort mode since its NSF project ended in August 2021. In September 2025 we announced on horus-ci.org that OSiRIS and HORUS would stop on 31 August 2026. That did not happen: both services are running, and they will continue on a best-effort basis through February 2027.

**What best effort means**

- The services stay up and we keep maintaining them, but there is no guaranteed availability or response time.
- Support continues by email: horus-help@umich.edu.
- Keep your own copy of any data you can't afford to lose.

**Looking beyond February 2027**

We may move the University of Michigan hardware to another location during February 2027, so that best-effort service can continue after that. Details, including any downtime, will be posted on horus-ci.org as soon as they are confirmed. If your work depends on OSiRIS after February 2027, please tell us at horus-help@umich.edu.

The full update is on horus-ci.org: [OSiRIS and HORUS continue, best effort, through February 2027](https://horus-ci.org/news/2026/11/09/service-update-february-2027).
